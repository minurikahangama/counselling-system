About Us

Welcome to Sri Lanka Sumithrayo

At Sri Lanka Sumithrayo, our volunteers are available for befriending via Telephone, Face to Face, Messaging, or Email at our centers. Opening and closing times at our centers vary from 9am to 4pm.

There are 10 Sri Lanka Sumithrayo centers spread across the country. Our Rural Program includes 2 centers in Panduwasnuwara & Lunugamverhera.

Sri Lanka Sumithrayo is a government-approved charity founded in 1974 by late Mrs. Joan De Mel.

It was incorporated by Act of Parliament No.10 of 1986.Sri Lanka Sumithrayo is a registered member of Befrienders Worldwide.

Providing emotional support to help those at risk of suicide.If you feel emotionally overwhelmed, please know that we are here for you.
